# ListenLab — A Peer-Based Mental Health Support & Training Platform

**A safe, non-clinical space connecting trained psychology students with people seeking compassionate, anonymous support.**

---

## 🌍 Overview

ListenLab is a peer-based mental health support and training platform designed to safely connect people seeking emotional support with trained psychology and social work students.

The platform addresses two connected gaps:

- the lack of accessible, responsible spaces for people to talk openly about their mental wellbeing,
- and the shortage of real-world, supervised practice opportunities for students in mental health–related fields.

ListenLab operates as a **non-clinical, safety-first environment**. Student listeners complete mandatory training in active listening, ethics, cultural sensitivity, and crisis recognition before engaging with help-seekers. In parallel, help-seekers access a calm, anonymous space where they can choose when and how to talk, without pressure, diagnosis, or judgment.

By combining structured training, reflective practice, peer community, and formal portfolio documentation, ListenLab transforms peer support into a meaningful educational experience while providing a responsible, compassionate resource for mental wellbeing.

---

## 🎯 Purpose — Why ListenLab Exists

ListenLab was created to address a structural mismatch in mental health support and education.

On one side, **many people need someone to talk to**, but face barriers such as cost, stigma, long waiting times, or discomfort with formal therapy. They are not always in crisis, yet they still need to be heard in a safe, respectful, and confidential space.

On the other side, **psychology students are expected to develop empathic and listening skills**, yet their training is often heavily theoretical. Meaningful, real-world practice is often delayed or limited.

ListenLab bridges this gap.

The platform allows psychology students to **practice active listening in a structured, supervised, and ethically bounded environment**, while offering help-seekers **free, anonymous, non-clinical emotional support**.

Importantly, ListenLab does **not** replace therapy or professional care. Instead, it provides a clearly defined peer-support space with strong safety protocols and escalation pathways.

## 🧩 How ListenLab Works

### 1. Two User Roles

**Help-Seekers**

- Anonymous access (username only)
- No diagnosis, no advice, no pressure
- Conversations can be ended at any time

**Student Listeners**

- Psychology and related students
- Verified registration
- Mandatory training before live sessions
- Progressive access based on engagement

---

### 2. Mandatory Training Before Going Live

Student listeners must complete **six core training modules**:

- Active Listening Essentials
- Empathy & Emotional Validation
- Cultural Sensitivity
- De-escalation Basics
- Ethical Boundaries
- Crisis Awareness (Non-clinical)

Training emphasizes **clear limits**: listeners are not therapists, do not diagnose, and do not give advice.

---

### 3. Matching & Conversations

- Help-seekers select _Find a Listener_
- Matched with available trained students
- Fully anonymous, in-platform chat
- Focus on reflection, validation, and presence

---

### 4. Reflection, Feedback & Progress Tracking

Student learning is reinforced through:

- Structured reflections
- XP points
- Levels
- Badges
- Session counts and listening hours
- Forum participation

Progress is earned through **real interaction**, not passive completion.

---

### 5. Professional Portfolio

Each student has a **personal portfolio page** displaying:

- Earned badges
- XP and levels
- Listening hours and sessions
- Reflections
- Practiced skills (e.g. empathy, boundaries)

Portfolios can be **exported as a PDF** (planned feature).

---

### 6. University Integration

Partner institutions can:

- Integrate ListenLab into curricula
- Monitor progress via instructor dashboards
- Access practicum-style reports
- Deploy ListenLab as a campus wellness tool

## 🛡️ Safety, Ethics & Clinical Boundaries

### Non-Clinical Scope

ListenLab does **not** provide therapy, diagnosis, or treatment.

### Clear Role Boundaries

Listeners are trained to validate, not advise or interpret.

### Crisis Awareness & Redirection

Not a crisis service — emergency resources are clearly provided.

### Privacy & Anonymity

Anonymous help-seekers, verified students, confidential sessions.

### Listener Wellbeing

Reflection, breaks, and peer support encouraged.

### Ethical Design Philosophy

Harm reduction, accountability, and psychological safety first.

## 🧰 Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** React-based framework

- **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS
- **Real-Time Communication:** Live chat infrastructure

## 🧪 How to Test the Application

Testing ListenLab requires **two users**:

1. **Help-Seeker**
   - Create a help-seeker account (username only)
   - Join the chat queue when ready to start a session

2. **Student Listener**
   - Create a student account with a verified email
   - Complete all **six training modules**
   - Once training is complete, start accepting chats
   - Connect with the waiting help-seeker in the queue

> Once both users are online, chat sessions will begin, demonstrating full functionality of the peer-support system.
